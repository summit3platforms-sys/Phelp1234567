Why This Happens

The main cause of grinding and skipping is a dry guide bar. In dusty places, dust mixes with old grease. This makes a thick, sticky dirt. This makes the parts drag. The motor fights this drag. The driving belt can then skip on the gears. This causes a loud grinding sound. Fixing problems early saves a lot of money. A quick weekly or monthly cleaning schedule helps a lot. Step-by-Step Fix

  1. Immediate Shutdown: Turn the printer off right away if you hear loud grinding. Letting the printer grind will permanently damage the motor gears.
  2. Inspect and Clean the Guide Bar: Open the top cover. Find the shiny metal guide bar. Use a clean cloth with isopropyl alcohol. Wipe away all old grease, dust, and dirt.
  3. Apply Proper Lubrication: Put on a very thin layer of printer lubricant. Use a high-quality, lightweight synthetic oil. You can also use a special carriage grease approved by Dascom.
  4. Check the Carriage Belt Tension: Look at the timing belt that moves the print head. It should be tight, but not too tight. Check closely for missing teeth or bad wear.
  5. Verify the Ribbon Routing (Dot Matrix): Make sure the ribbon cartridge sits perfectly flat on an impact printer. Check that the ribbon is routed correctly.
  6. Adjust the Print Head Gap: The gap might be too tight for thick paper. Move the lever or dial to a wider setting. This stops the head from dragging.
  7. Test Movement Without Media: Turn the printer on without paper or ribbon. Watch the startup sequence to ensure smooth movement.

Advanced Troubleshooting

Cleaning the guide bar and adjusting the belt might not stop skipping. If so, the problem could be the optical encoder strip. This is a thin, clear plastic strip behind the carriage. It has hundreds of tiny vertical lines. A sensor on the carriage reads these lines. This tells the printer where the print head is. The problem might be bad firmware (internal software) if all steps fail. A power drop can sometimes mess up the internal memory. Try a 'hard' factory reset. This often means pressing certain buttons while turning the printer on. This clears bad data and returns the printer to factory settings. You can then set it up clean.

FAQ

Can I use WD-40 to lubricate the printer carriage?

Absolutely not. Standard WD-40 is a cleaner, not a long-term oil. It will dry up fast and leave a sticky film.

The carriage slams into the side of the printer loudly. What is wrong?

This is usually caused by a dirty or loose encoder strip. The printer cannot read its location. It keeps moving the motor until it hits the side.

Why are only half of the letters printing clearly on my dot matrix printer?

The print head likely has broken or jammed pins if dots are missing. The print head gap might be wrong if the whole letter is light.
